How the sync works
  • Onsets come from spectral flux in three bands (bass, mid, high) with an adaptive threshold. There is no BPM and no grid.
  • Pulse: the stronger onsets are picked by local context, so tempo drift, rubato and odd meters are kept as they are. Every move eases from one real hit to the next.
  • Sections: changes in energy and onset density split the song. Calm stretches become the sincere scenes, and cuts are snapped onto strong hits.
  • Words in the speech bubbles appear on onsets inside the phrase, so the lines feel sung.
